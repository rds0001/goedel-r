# goedel 0.2.0

* Add Q and PA axiom presentations, arithmetic parsing, exact numeral notation,
  and induction-schema construction and recognition.
* Add explicit finite structures, Kripke models, complete finite frame valuation
  checks, and natural deduction with scopes, discharge and equality.
* Add exact natural-sequence and shared arithmetic DAG coding, capture-avoiding
  DAG substitution, and separate versioned scoped and Hilbert proof transport.
* Add native exact register execution, independent trace replay, concrete
  arithmetic fixed points with an explicit trusted representation instance,
  and the fixed PA Hilbert checker and predicate-specific Pr/Con formulas.
* Add typed conditional catalogue inference with independent witness replay,
  twelve research topics and an optional lossless igraph adapter.
* Preserve the original syntax numbering, proof fragment and bounded-search APIs.
  No native check is presented as a formally verified kernel or consistency proof.
